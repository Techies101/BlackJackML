# Design brief: "21 Vision: AI Card Counter" app logo and icon

## What you are designing

A logo / app icon for **21 Vision: AI Card Counter**, a Windows desktop tool that watches an online live-dealer blackjack table, counts cards (Hi-Lo) and advises the player. The icon appears in the app's custom title bar, the Windows taskbar and the program's window icon.

## Concept

- A **King** and a **Queen** (as on playing cards: face-card royalty, crowned figures), placed symmetrically, one on each side.
- The number **"21"** in the centre, between them, as the focal point (blackjack's winning total, and the "21" of the app name).
- The feel: a premium casino card, reimagined in a **dark + neon** style: sharp, modern and techy (it is an AI tool), not old-fashioned or ornate.

Suggested composition (you may improve on it): a rounded-square or card-shaped badge; the King's and Queen's crowned heads/busts facing inward from the left and right edges (like the mirrored halves of a face card); a bold "21" centred and overlapping them slightly.

## Style (must match the app)

- **Theme: dark + neon.** Near-black background `#07090C` or a charcoal card `#0E1117`, with neon line work and accents.
- Neon palette used by the app (one meaning each; pick 2–3 for the logo):
  - Neon green `#3DFF8F` (primary brand accent, "live")
  - Cyan `#38D6FF` (selection)
  - Magenta `#FF3DD0` (highlight)
  - Yellow `#FFE24A` (gold-like accent, suitable for crowns)
  - Red `#FF4D6D` (card red, e.g. a heart/diamond detail)
- Glow should be suggested with **solid bright colours and crisp outlines**, not blur, gradients-with-transparency or drop shadows: the icon must stay sharp at tiny sizes and in the Windows .ico format.
- Typeface feel for "21": bold and condensed, like **Barlow Condensed Bold** (the app's numeral font), or a custom numeral in that spirit.

## Hard constraints

1. **Readable at 16 × 16 and 20 × 20 px** (the title bar shows it at about 18 px). At those sizes the King and Queen can't be detailed, so provide a **simplified small-size variant**: e.g. just the neon "21" with a tiny crown above it on the dark rounded square. The full King + Queen version is for 48 px and up.
2. **Square**, with a little padding so the mark doesn't touch the edges.
3. Works on the app's dark title bar (`#0B0E13`) **and** on light Windows taskbars (hence the solid dark badge behind the mark).
4. No text other than "21" (the app name appears next to the icon in the title bar).

## Deliverables

- **Master logo** as **SVG** (full King + Queen + 21 version).
- **Small-size variant** as **SVG** (simplified, for 16–32 px).
- **PNG exports**: 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and 512 px (small variant for 16–32, full version for 48 and up).
- **Windows .ico** containing 16, 20, 24, 32, 48 and 256 px layers.
- A preview sheet showing: the icon in a dark title bar next to the text "21 Vision: AI Card Counter" at 18 px, the icon at every PNG size, and on light and dark backgrounds.

## Keep in mind

- The app's title bar is 32 px high, dark (`#0B0E13`), with the title "21 Vision: AI Card Counter" in white Barlow Bold 13 px to the right of the icon.
- Avoid real casino brand marks or copyrighted card-deck artwork; the King and Queen should be original drawings.
